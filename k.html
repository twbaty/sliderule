Slide Rule Topic 3

Learn the K scale.

K is the cube scale. It extends the logarithmic idea to cube roots and cubes, making it useful for volume, scaling laws, and any place where three powers show up.

Hub C and D A and B K CI and DI L S and T
Core idea

Cubing and cube roots.

The K scale usually spreads over three decades. That makes it perfect for cube-related problems when the answer spans a wider range than the ordinary C/D pair.

  • Use it when you need a cube or cube root.
  • The same decimal-place judgement still applies.
  • It is often a favorite for geometry and engineering estimates.
Practice

Look for the pattern

1. Start with a number like 2 or 3.
2. Think about its cube.
3. Then reverse it and see how cube roots land back on the scale.

The important part is not memorizing a formula. It is getting comfortable with the way the scale spacing compresses or expands values.

Why it matters

Three powers, same logic.

K is a good reminder that slide rules are a family of logarithmic tools. Once you understand the idea, the labels change but the method stays familiar.

Next step
