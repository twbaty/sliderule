Slide Rule Topic 6

Learn the S and T scales.

S and T are the classic trig scales. They let you work with sines and tangents in the same tactile way that C and D handle multiplication.

Hub C and D A and B K CI and DI L S and T
Core idea

Angles become scale positions.

The S and T scales help you read trigonometric relationships directly. Instead of reaching for a table, you align the scale and read the corresponding angle or function value.

  • S is commonly associated with sine work.
  • T is commonly associated with tangent work.
  • The same rule about estimating and checking still applies.
Practice

Start with a familiar angle

1. Pick a simple angle like 30 or 45 degrees.
2. Find the approximate position on the trig scale.
3. Compare the value to what you would expect from memory or a table.

Trig scales feel more magical at first, but the workflow is the same: set, read, and estimate before you trust the decimal place.

Why it matters

Trig without leaving the instrument.

The trig scales show how far slide rules could go. They are a good last stop because they pull together the whole idea of reading a function from a physical scale.

Finished the path

Loop back when you want to review.

After trig, go back to C and D, because that is where the whole family starts. Once those feel natural, the other scales become much easier to remember.
