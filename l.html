Slide Rule Topic 5

Learn the L scale.

The L scale is a logarithm scale. It is not as common on every rule, but it is a useful bridge between the slide rule's physical movement and the math it represents.

Hub C and D A and B K CI and DI L S and T
Core idea

Read the logarithm directly.

The L scale can show logarithmic values more directly than the ordinary multiplication scales. That makes it useful in a teaching context for showing why the slide rule works at all.

  • Best seen as a math bridge, not just a calculator shortcut.
  • It helps connect physical distance to logarithmic value.
  • Very useful for understanding the broader logic of the rule.
Practice

Think in powers

1. Ask what value the spacing would represent in log terms.
2. Compare it with the ordinary C and D spacing.
3. Notice how a direct log reading makes the curve of the rule easier to trust.

This is the kind of scale that rewards curiosity. It is less about speed and more about insight.

Why it matters

Seeing the math behind the tool.

Many learners first meet logs through the slide rule, not the other way around. L scales are a good reminder that the instrument is really a logarithm machine.
