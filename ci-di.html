Slide Rule Topic 4

Learn the CI and DI scales.

The inverse scales flip the ordinary C and D scales around. That can make reciprocal work feel much easier, especially when one factor is awkward to place on the normal scale.

Hub C and D A and B K CI and DI L S and T
Core idea

Flip the scale, keep the logic.

CI and DI are inverse versions of C and D. Instead of reading from 1 upward in the usual direction, the scale is reversed, which makes some reciprocal calculations shorter.

  • Very handy for division and awkward factors.
  • Often easier than constantly moving the cursor back and forth.
  • Great practice for seeing the same math from another angle.
Practice

Use the reverse idea

1. Find the reciprocal-friendly setup.
2. Notice how the placement changes, but the reading logic does not.
3. Compare the shortcut to the standard C/D method.

Once you can see why an inverse scale saves time, the rest of the rule family starts to feel much less mysterious.

Why it matters

Some problems are easier from the right side.

The point of inverse scales is not to replace the normal scales. It is to give you a better starting point when the factor you need is awkward or hidden.
